Dataset Notes:
  • Values recorded as -1 within the arrays indicate that the specific measurement was skipped, unmeasured, or deemed inapplicable for that particular sample.
  • Samples M5g and M5h represent suspended sediment samples collected directly from the river using water bottles, and thus follow a different collection protocol compared to standard bed material samples.
  • Specific Gravity (Gs) Assignments: Actual specific gravity measurements (using a pycnometer) were only conducted on representative samples M1, M3a, and M5a. Assuming that nearby sediments share similar physical properties, these measured Specific_Gravity_Gs values were assigned to adjacent samples based on spatial proximity as follows:
    • M1 data assigned to: 5, 6A, 6B, 6C, M1, M2.
    • M3a data assigned to: 1, 2, 3, 4, M3a, M3b, M3c, M4.
    • M5a data assigned to: M5a, M5b, M5c, M5d, M5e, M5f, M5g, M5h.
